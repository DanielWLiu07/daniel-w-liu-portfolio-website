# Casino delivery assets

Keep authored inputs and the older delivery files intact. The public scene uses:

- `KatieRoze-display-512.woff2`: generated by `python3 scripts/optimize-casino-display-font.py`. All glyphs, advance widths and outlines retained; the 911-ppem embedded bitmap strike becomes 512 ppem with a 256-entry RGBA palette. This is a visual delivery tradeoff, not lossless compression. Current canvas lettering is drawn at 130–190 px. Original 911-ppem artwork remains in `KatieRoze-web.woff2`.
- `watercolor_normal-delivery.webp`: `node scripts/optimize-casino-delivery.mjs`. Same 1024×1024 dimensions, near-lossless WebP, measured RGB RMSE below 2/255. Authored PNG and lossless WebP remain available.
- Dealer: run `node scripts/optimize-casino-dealer.mjs --delivery` then `node scripts/compress-casino-dealer.mjs --delivery`. Texture uses WebP86 at its original 2048×2048 size (RGB/alpha RMSE3.49/255). Meshopt now includes sparse morph-target indices/values: 232 compressed views, checked byte-exact with both encoder-package and shipped runtime decoders. No geometry simplification or animation/rigging changes. Raw GLB3.94→2.99MB; gzip estimate2.95→2.42MB. Intermediate delivery GLB/manifest stay local; authored, compact and lossless sources are retained.

The resume JPEG and PDF are deliberately unchanged: lossless WebP conversion of the JPEG was larger, not smaller.
